## Purpose

This file is the durable source of truth for the botanical placement programme. Every listed asset is a separate composition, not a crop, rotation or mirror of another asset. Future generation must proceed one family at a time and stop for review after that family is packaged.

## Universal production rules

Every asset must be an original, abundant, connected botanical arrangement rendered as refined hand-painted invitation artwork with realistic watercolor/gouache detail. It must have a genuine transparent alpha background and clean antialiased edges. Flowers, buds, leaves and stems must form one believable plant structure. Vertical, horizontal and corner placements must be authored for their named position.

Never include paper, color wash, glow, haze, shadow plane, frame, vase, ribbon, text, watermark, isolated bloom, floating leaf, severed stem, fused blossom, malformed petals, impossible plant anatomy, awkward bend, hard ninety-degree stem, sparse incidental sprig, mirrored duplicate or rotated reuse. PNG is the review master; WebP must be lossless and preserve RGBA transparency.
