## Sources and review boundary

Lexical checks were made against the digitized Monier-Williams Sanskrit-English Dictionary and its cross-indexed entries: `vivāha`, `pariṇaya`, `udvāha`, `vivāhavidhi`, `vivāhotsava`, `pāṇigrahaṇa`, and `saptapadī`. A word being attested does not establish it as an appropriate Mauritian wedding invitation heading. Have a fluent Sanskrit reader and the family's officiant review spelling, inflection, pronunciation and ceremony suitability. Do not use ritual-specific titles as a generic wedding label unless the actual ceremony matches.
