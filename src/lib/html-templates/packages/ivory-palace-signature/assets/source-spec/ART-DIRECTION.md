# Art Direction

## Design thesis

Ivory Palace is one continuous ceremonial journey through a fictional Indo-palatial estate. It is not a component catalogue. Architecture, florals, light, perspective, and colour progress from dawn warmth into evening candlelight while retaining one material vocabulary.

## Continuity rules

- Front-facing cusped arches, ivory carved stone, fine floral relief, and antique gold repeat across scenes.
- Light originates from upper-left in day scenes; lamps add low warm pools at dusk.
- Roses, jasmine, marigold, and mango leaves appear selectively. No scene uses every species.
- Burgundy increases toward Sangeet, Reception, and finale; marigold dominates only Haldi; green dominates only Mehendi.
- Sacred imagery remains optional and absent from this base artwork.
- Live text always sits on naturally quiet architecture, sky, water, or floor—not a card.
