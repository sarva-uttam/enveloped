# Licensing and Provenance

Every asset and font needs its creator, source, creation method, licence, permitted uses, modification rights, attribution needs and evidence location. “Found online” is not provenance. Do not commit copyrighted references without permission.

Fonts remain `TO_VERIFY` until the exact family, version, source URL, licence text and required script coverage are confirmed. Client-provided photographs or music require an explicit rights declaration and scoped access controls. Approval is blocked when provenance or licence evidence is missing.
