The initial market is Mauritian Hindu weddings. Survey profiles are Mauritian Hindu, Mauritian Tamil Hindu, mixed Hindu traditions, not sure, Let Enveloped advise me, and no sacred imagery. International cultural collections are deferred until suitable review.
