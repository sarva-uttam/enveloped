## Shared production contract

- Original sRGB artwork; no invitation text, fake script, signature, watermark, logo, accidental sacred symbol or third-party pixels.
- Transparent components require genuine clean alpha, generous padding, complete unclipped subjects and no coloured matte or halo.
- Visual language: refined editorial illustration with warm ivory, antique/champagne gold, restrained deep red, jasmine white, marigold orange and peacock teal accents.
- Directional light: soft warm upper-centre source. Grounded subjects use a small physically plausible contact shadow contained within alpha.
- Central text-safe area: x 170–830, y 330–1160. Decorative assets must not reduce readable contrast or cover primary text.
- Master artwork is at least twice intended rendered size. Required exports per raster asset: lossless PNG master, alpha WebP delivery, 540×960 option card, 270×480 thumbnail and full-resolution detail crop.
- Every output begins `UNDER_REVIEW`; malformed anatomy, construction, support, attachment, symmetry, alpha or perspective causes rejection, never silent repair.
- Trial compatibility: `HW-BG-001` PALE, `HW-ARCH-001` v2, `HW-HEADER-001`, `PAL-IVORY-GOLD`, H08-compatible slots. `HW-FLORAL-010-V1-BASE` is an existing UNDER_REVIEW trial dependency, not an approved production asset.
