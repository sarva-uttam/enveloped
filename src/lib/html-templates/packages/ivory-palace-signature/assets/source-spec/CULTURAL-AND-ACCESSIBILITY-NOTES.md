# Cultural and Accessibility Notes

- Base scenes intentionally contain no Om, Ganesha, mantra, or client-specific blessing. Sacred headers remain optional and require explicit client/Owner selection.
- No sacred image may become a button, sit below footwear, be cropped, receive particle effects, or appear beneath controls.
- Elephants in the entrance artwork are architectural guardian sculptures, not interactive or disposable motifs. A culturally fluent reviewer should confirm their appropriateness for the intended family/tradition.
- Haldi vessels are decorative and must not be described as a universal ritual prescription. Ceremony wording and object accuracy require Mauritian Hindu review.
- Mixed Hindu, Tamil Hindu, “not sure,” and advisory cases require human cultural review. “No sacred imagery” is a complete path.
- Proposed alt text: scenes carrying no unique content should be decorative (`alt=""`). Meaningful finale artwork: “An illustrated newlywed couple in burgundy and ivory attire stands on an illuminated palace terrace.” Gallery photos use client-provided alt text.
- Text contrast: use `#34251F` on light quiet fields and `#FFF6E8` on dusk fields, with a local contrast scrim only if it is a feathered colour-grade layer—not a visible card. Verify WCAG AA against the final crop.
- Controls require 44 × 44 CSS-pixel targets, visible keyboard focus, semantic anchors/buttons, and labels independent of artwork.
- Reading order is DOM order, not visual layer order. Animation cannot be required to reveal content. Reduced-motion mode is complete and static.

Human review flags: entrance elephant symbolism; Haldi vessels; any later sacred header; any Sanskrit/Hindi/Tamil text; ceremony naming and sequence for the specific families.
