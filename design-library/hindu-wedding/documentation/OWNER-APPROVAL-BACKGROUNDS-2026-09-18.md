# Owner Approval — Background Families — 18 September 2026

The Owner approved all 44 colourways across the eleven registered Hindu Wedding background families after reviewing individual 9:16 survey previews, four-mood family comparison cards, tier contact sheets and the complete contact sheet.

The approved families are:

- Bronze: HW-BG-001 Ivory Whisper, HW-BG-002 Champagne Parchment, HW-BG-004 Sage Serenity.
- Silver: HW-BG-003 Blush Petal Wash, HW-BG-005 Ruby Velvet, HW-BG-007 Peacock Teal.
- Gold: HW-BG-006 Gilded Sandstone, HW-BG-010 Rosewater Cloud, HW-BG-011 Amber Reverie.
- Platinum: HW-BG-008 Temple Ember, HW-BG-009 Golden Veil.

Each family contains `PALE`, `SUBTLE`, `DEEP` and `DARK`. All four moods inherit their parent's minimum tier. Browsing colourways is free; only the active selected component or applicable tier is charged. No reusable background is Bespoke.

Nine technically or visually unsuitable first-pass candidates were rejected and replaced before approval. Rejected files are excluded from the repository and cannot be selected.

This approval applies only to backgrounds. It does not approve architectural frames, sacred imagery, flowers, later components or assembled templates.
