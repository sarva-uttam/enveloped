# Haldi titles — revised shortlist

Owner decision: HT-02, HT-04, HT-05, HT-07 and HT-12 rejected. The seven retained titles and five new Roman-letter Hindi/Hindustani titles (HT-13–HT-17) are approved as editable wording choices. Hindi language and cultural use still require fluent review before publication. Original IDs are never reassigned.

| ID | Title | Status | Register and usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HT-01 | Haldi Ceremony | Approved | Clear formal English; safest default. |
| HT-03 | Haldi & Blessings | Approved | Gentle; confirm blessings suit the family. |
| HT-06 | Rang De Haldi | Approved | Playful Hindi/Hinglish from the source; celebratory colour cue, not Sanskrit. |
| HT-08 | Haldi Ka Jashn | Approved | Informal Hindi/Hindustani, “a celebration of Haldi.” |
| HT-09 | Haldi Ki Khushiyan | Approved | Lyrical Hindi, “the joys of Haldi.” |
| HT-10 | Sunehri Haldi | Approved | Creative Hindi, “golden Haldi”; a colour metaphor. |
| HT-11 | हल्दी समारोह | Approved | Hindi Devanagari, “Haldi ceremony”; formal. |
| HT-13 | Haldi Ke Rang | Approved | Hindi in Roman letters, “the colours of Haldi”; expressive and visual. |
| HT-14 | Haldi Ki Raunak | Approved | Hindi/Urdu register, “the glow or liveliness of Haldi”; festive. |
| HT-15 | Haldi Ki Mehfil | Approved | Hindi/Urdu register, “a Haldi gathering”; suggests a social celebration. |
| HT-16 | Haldi Ki Rasam | Approved | Hindi/Urdu register, “the Haldi ritual”; use when the ritual is the focus. |
| HT-17 | Khushiyon Ki Haldi | Approved | Hindi in Roman letters, “a Haldi of joys”; playful, fluent idiom review especially important. |

Rejected IDs remain in the decision history, never in the selectable shortlist: HT-02 The Haldi Celebration; HT-04 A Golden Beginning; HT-05 The Golden Haldi; HT-07 Haldi Utsav; HT-12 हल्दी उत्सव · Haldi Celebration.

Component meanings are checked against the Hindwi dictionary. Creative compounds are editorial titles, not claims of established ritual names. Hindi/Hinglish titles, including previously Owner-approved choices, still need fluent human review for naturalness and local register before client publication.
