# Tier and Pricing Model

Custom invitations progress Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum. Bronze remains complete and professional. Higher tiers add luxury, animation, customisation, production effort and component choice; premium features are never required to finish an invitation.

## Approved base prices

| Region | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mauritius | Rs 990 | Rs 2,490 | Rs 3,990 | Rs 6,490 |
| Standard international | US$59 | US$95 | US$139 | US$209 |

Regional groups may later include affordable international and higher-income markets. The billing country determines availability. Purchasing power—not exchange rate alone—must guide country grouping. Group membership and unapproved prices remain an Owner decision.

## Approved add-ons

| Region | Included | Enhanced | Premium | Bespoke |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Mauritius | Rs 0 | Rs 75–150 | Rs 200–400 | Quotation required |
| International | US$0 | US$2–5 | US$8–16 | Quotation required |

Browsing never changes price. Only an active selection is charged, and replacement removes the earlier charge. If the current tier plus additions costs more than the next tier containing the same features, recommend the next tier without changing it automatically.

Fixed-price ready-made templates remain a separate product. Their individual prices are still pending.
