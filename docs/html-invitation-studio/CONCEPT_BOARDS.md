## 1–3. Concept boards, desktop/mobile direction, and opening experience

### 1.1 Timeless Editorial Wedding

**Board.** Cotton-paper stationery, ink-on-ecru, one confident serif, a single sprig monogram as the only recurring motif. The page should feel like it was set on a letterpress, not rendered.

**Palette.**
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` | `#F5EEE2` | ground |
| `ink` | `#211C17` | primary text |
| `ink-soft` | `#5B5148` | secondary text |
| `accent-rose` | `#B4676B` | ornament/divider (validated alt: `accent-sage` `#7C8B6F`) |
| `line` | `#DCD1BE` | hairline rules |

**Typography.** Display: **Canela** or, as an open-license equivalent, **Fraunces** (variable, supports the soft-serif italic emphasis this concept needs) at large sizes/italic for names. Body/logistics: **Inter** or **Public Sans** at small size, wide-tracked uppercase for eyebrow labels. Two faces only, both self-hostable under their respective licenses (Fraunces/Inter are OFL/open).

**Desktop concept board.** Centered column, max-width ~680px, sitting in generous asymmetric negative space (more margin right than left, a deliberate imbalance rather than dead-center symmetry). Hairline rule + sprig mark between every section. The couple-portrait section is the one place the column breaks wider — near-full-bleed within the page, not edge-to-edge of the viewport.

**Mobile concept board.** Same column logic at full width, tighter but still generous margins (min 24px side gutter). The portrait section keeps its full-bleed treatment — the single place mobile and desktop composition converge, since a full-bleed photo reads the same at any width.

**Opening experience.** No envelope. Page loads on paper texture; a single centered line ("The wedding of—") cross-fades/rises (600–800ms, ease-out, no bounce) into the couple's names set in the display face. A typographic reveal, not a click-to-unseal mechanic.

**Decorative system.** One monogram/sprig SVG mark in 2–3 rotations (opening, section dividers, closing), a single hairline-rule primitive, no border/frame system — restraint is the ornament.

**Motion direction.** Scroll-triggered opacity/translate-Y on section entry only; one deliberate cross-fade at open; full collapse to instant-visible under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### 1.2 Majestic Hindu Wedding

**Board.** Layered ornamental frame around nested content panels, jewel-tone ground with sparing genuine-metallic-gold linework, floral/paisley motifs distinct from the border system. The bar: specific and respectful, not a costume-party "ethnic" reskin.

**Palette (validated set — admin selects one per invite, not an open hex field).**
| Set | Ground | Accent | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maroon | `#5A1A1F` | `#D4AF6A` (gold) | `#F7EEDD` |
| Deep teal | `#0F3D3E` | `#D4AF6A` (gold) | `#F3EFE3` |
| Ivory & gold | `#F6EEDD` | `#B08A3E` (gold) | `#2B2118` |

**Typography.** Display: **Cormorant** (or **Cinzel** for the crest/monogram lockup) for names — genuine calligraphic warmth without faking Devanagari character with a Latin face. Body/logistics: **Mukta** or **Poppins**. If a client supplies regional-language text, it renders in **Noto Serif Devanagari** (or the correct Noto Serif variant for the script supplied) — a properly OFL-licensed, self-hostable font family with real script support, never a transliteration styled to "look" like the script.

**Desktop concept board.** Wide framed canvas — the border system needs real room to read as architecture (a mandap-style arch, corner motifs, edge repeats) rather than a squeezed strip. Ceremony-sequence panels (haldi/sangeet/wedding/reception — only whichever the client actually supplies) lay out as a horizontal sequence when there are 2 or more.

**Mobile concept board.** The border is **redesigned**, not shrunk: a top-and-bottom ornament band plus side hairlines replaces the full frame, so detail survives at narrow width. Ceremony panels stack vertically. This is the highest mobile-adaptation-risk concept of the three and needs its own dedicated mobile artwork pass — flagged explicitly for the asset manifest below.

**Opening experience.** The outer frame (border SVG) draws/fades in first; central panel content resolves inside it after — evoking unfolding a physical card through layered opacity/scale on real vector artwork. No CSS-drawn envelope flap.

**Decorative system.** Coordinated border/frame pieces (corner motifs, edge repeat, optional central crest), a separate paisley/floral motif set, an optional deity/auspicious-symbol motif **only if the client's tradition calls for it and admin-selects it** — never hardcoded to one tradition or denomination.

**Motion direction.** Border-draw-in on open; gentle panel-by-panel scroll reveal (settle, no bounce/spring); a restrained CSS-only shimmer on gold linework at the crest — subtle, not glittery.

**Cultural note carried forward from the architecture doc:** no sacred/ritual wording is ever invented by the template; any mantra or blessing line is client-supplied verbatim or omitted. This concept should not go to a real client without a cultural-fluency review pass.

### 1.3 Royal Evening Celebration

**Board.** Dark, formal, poster-like — large-scale type as the primary anchor, engraved-linework crest, near-black ground with warm gold/champagne accents. Closer to a black-tie program cover than wedding stationery.

**Palette.**
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `charcoal` | `#15130F` | ground |
| `champagne` | `#C9A86A` | linework, type accent |
| `accent-burgundy` | `#5E1A26` | one reserved graphic element only |
| `text-high` | `#F2ECDF` | body on dark |

**Typography.** Display: **Fraunces** at didone-leaning settings (high contrast cut) or **Cormorant SC** for the headline moment, set very large. Logistics: a monospaced/wide-tracked sans — **Space Mono** or **JetBrains Mono** at small size — the technical pairing is what gives this concept its "formal program" identity, distinct from both other concepts' warmer serif/sans pairings.

**Desktop concept board.** True poster asymmetry — headline type genuinely off-center, generous dark negative space doing real compositional work, logistics block positioned deliberately (e.g. lower-right), not centered.

**Mobile concept board.** The asymmetric composition **does not survive narrow viewports as-is** and is deliberately re-composed: centered, top-aligned title treatment at mobile widths. This is a planned divergence in layout logic between breakpoints, not a naive reflow — flagged for its own mobile composition pass.

**Opening experience.** No envelope, no card metaphor. Page opens on the textured dark ground; the headline title animates in via a slow type-scale-and-tracking resolve (letters settle from expanded tracking to final tracking — an engraved plate coming into focus).

**Decorative system.** One precisely-drawn line-art crest/monogram (fine engraved linework, not a bold clipart badge), a subtle dark brushed-texture ground, one full-bleed dramatic photo slot treated dark/high-contrast.

**Motion direction.** The most minimal of the three — one slow type-resolve on open, otherwise near-static; any scroll motion is a simple slow opacity settle, nothing kinetic.
