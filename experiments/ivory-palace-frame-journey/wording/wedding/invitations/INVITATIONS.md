# Dark green — ten bride-side parent invitation sentences

Status: All ten parent invitation sentences approved by Owner; editable per invitation. Display `{brideParents}` on a separate line, then one sentence below it, then the large `{brideName}`. The sentence must remain grammatically connected to the name line; the full accessible sentence combines all three segments. `daughter` is appropriate only when the confirmed hosts are her parents. Other host arrangements need their own editable wording.

| ID | Voice | Invitation lead after `{brideParents}` and before `{brideName}` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WI-01 | Classic | `warmly invite you to the wedding of their daughter` |
| WI-02 | Formal | `request the pleasure of your company at the wedding of their daughter` |
| WI-03 | Warm | `would be delighted to have you celebrate the wedding of their daughter` |
| WI-04 | Traditional | `with joy in their hearts, invite you to the wedding of their daughter` |
| WI-05 | Elegant | `invite you to share in the beautiful wedding celebration of their daughter` |
| WI-06 | Family-centred | `invite you to join their family for the wedding of their daughter` |
| WI-07 | Blessing-oriented | `invite you to bless and celebrate the wedding of their daughter` |
| WI-08 | Concise | `invite you to celebrate their daughter's wedding` |
| WI-09 | Affectionate | `would love for you to join them as their daughter is married` |
| WI-10 | Contemporary | `are thrilled to invite you to celebrate their daughter's wedding` |

For WI-01 through WI-07, the following separate bride name line completes the lead. WI-08 through WI-10 are already complete sentences; the bride name beneath is an identification display rather than a grammatical completion. The layout must never add a second name inside the lead. A no-parentage option for other structures is `{hosts} invite you to celebrate the wedding of {brideName} and {groomName}.` The invitation may instead be issued by the groom's parents, both families, the couple or another confirmed host; do not silently substitute “their daughter” in those cases.
